# Iyu.UBoard.EntityFrameworkCore

Entity Framework Core board store behind Iyu.UBoard - the boards, their revisions and their publication history - over the application's own DbContext tables.

```
dotnet add package Iyu.UBoard.EntityFrameworkCore
```

**Documentation:** [Keeping boards and their history (`Iyu.UBoard`)](https://github.com/iyulab/iyu-framework-v5#keeping-boards-and-their-history-iyuuboard) in the repository README.

`Iyu.UBoard.EntityFrameworkCore` is versioned with `Iyu.UBoard` on its own line, separate from the other `Iyu.*` packages of [iyu-framework-v5](https://github.com/iyulab/iyu-framework-v5); the [changelog](https://github.com/iyulab/iyu-framework-v5/blob/main/src/Iyu.UBoard/CHANGELOG.md) is shipped in this package.
